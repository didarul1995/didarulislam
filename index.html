I study how cities grow, heat, and adapt in a changing climate, and who carries the risk.

I am a Research Fellow at the University of Michigan School for Environment and Sustainability. My research connects urban expansion, urban heat, access to green space, and climate impacts on people, including displacement and migration. I combine optical, thermal, SAR, and LiDAR observations with explainable GeoAI to turn Earth observation into evidence for planning and policy.

My work includes the first 25 m Local Climate Zone map of the contiguous United States, annual 10 m canopy height and biomass across more than 170 million acres of California and Arizona and for the Sundarbans mangroves, and urban expansion mapping that informed Bangladesh's national urban policy. It has been supported by NASA, NOAA, NSF, the USDA Forest Service, and UK GCRF.

Research

Three connected questions organize my work. Each theme links to the papers behind it.

Cities in transition

How do cities expand, and what does that growth change on the ground?

  • Expansion, redevelopment, and land use change from satellite time series.
  • Building footprints, OpenStreetMap, census, and imagery fused into urban data cubes.
  • Urban form and heat exposure, from Dhaka to the contiguous U.S.

Climate, risk, and people

Who is exposed to climate change, who is displaced by it, and who can adapt?

  • Floods, landslides, severe weather, and national climate vulnerability.
  • Land and heat change around refugee settlements, and where growth can go.
  • Tree canopy deficits, cooling green space, and green displacement risk.

Ecosystems and methods

How can Earth observation measure change reliably and transparently?

  • Canopy height and biomass from GEDI and airborne LiDAR fusion.
  • Crop yield forecasting and a national agricultural digital twin.
  • Spatial machine learning with SHAP and GeoShapley, at scale.

Technical skills

The methods and tools behind my research, which I also bring to teaching in remote sensing, GeoAI, big geospatial data, cloud computing, and spatial cyberinfrastructure.

Remote sensing

Data: optical, SAR, LiDAR, thermal, and coarse resolution imagery (e.g., Sentinel-1 and 2, Landsat, NAIP, ALOS PALSAR, GEDI, ICESat-2, MODIS, VIIRS) and UAS imagery

Analysis: multi-sensor fusion, time series analysis, change detection, land surface temperature, and canopy height and biomass retrieval

Software: ESA SNAP, ENVI, ERDAS IMAGINE, Google Earth Engine

LiDAR
Airborne and spaceborne LiDAR processing (e.g., airborne laser scanning, GEDI, ICESat-2); canopy structure metrics and terrain models; automated point cloud workflows at state scale
Machine learning and GeoAI

Machine learning: ensemble and classical models (e.g., random forest, XGBoost), unsupervised clustering, AutoML, and hyperparameter optimization

Deep learning: CNNs (e.g., ResNet, U-Net, Mask R-CNN, YOLO); foundation models (e.g., Segment Anything); sequence models (e.g., LSTM); generative models (e.g., pix2pix); spatial transfer learning

Explainable AI: model interpretation for spatial prediction (e.g., SHAP, GeoShapley)

Spatial statistics and analysis
Spatial regression and spatially varying coefficient models; spatial autocorrelation and pattern analysis; spatial cross-validation; network, accessibility, and location-allocation analysis
Programming
Python and R with the full suite of GIS, remote sensing, spatial statistics, and GeoAI libraries, including explainable AI (XAI); SQL; JavaScript
GIS platforms
ArcGIS platform (e.g., ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Enterprise, ArcGIS Online, Dashboards); QGIS
Spatial cyberinfrastructure
Design and development of spatial databases and services (e.g., PostgreSQL with PostGIS, GeoServer, OGC web services); backend APIs (e.g., FastAPI, GeoDjango); automated ETL pipelines; containerized, version controlled workflows (e.g., Docker, Git)
Cloud and high-performance computing
Spatial computing on commercial cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud); Google Earth Engine at continental scale; HPC clusters with distributed and GPU computing; MLOps and model deployment
